✦ THE AZURE COAST ✦

Where the Sea Meets the Sky

≋ ✦ ≋

The Azure Coast is a vast coastal region stretching along the western edge of Aetheria.

Endless turquoise waters surround towering cliffs, secluded beaches, coral reefs, sea caves, and bustling harbor towns.

Ancient lighthouses stand against the waves while small islands scatter across the horizon like fragments of a forgotten kingdom.

The Coast is beautiful.

It is also unpredictable.

Storms can arrive without warning. The tides hide things beneath the water.

And sailors have learned that the brightest waters can conceal the deepest dangers.

✦ BEYOND THE HORIZON ✦

THE AZURE ISLES

≋ ✦ ≋

Hundreds of islands are scattered throughout the Azure Sea.

Some are inhabited.

Others have never been explored.

A few islands appear on maps one year and disappear from them the next.

🌙

Local sailors claim the sea itself sometimes changes the location of certain islands.

✦ BENEATH THE WAVES ✦

THE SUNKEN KINGDOM

≋ ───── ✦ ───── ≋

Far beneath the Azure Sea lies the remains of an ancient civilization.

Its name has been lost to time.

Massive marble towers, broken statues, and enormous gates can still be found beneath the water.

Some scholars believe the kingdom possessed powerful ocean magic.

Others believe it was destroyed by something beneath the sea.

🐚

No one has reached its deepest ruins and returned with a complete account.

✦ THE LEGEND OF THE AZURE COAST ✦

DARIUS VANE

THE DROWNED KING

≋ ✦ ≋ ───── ≋ ✦ ≋

Darius Vane is one of the most feared figures associated with the Azure Coast.

Known throughout the coastal kingdoms as The Drowned King, his name is spoken by sailors, merchants, pirates, and coastal villagers alike.

Some claim Darius commands a fleet that moves through the Azure Sea without leaving a wake.

Others say his ship appears only during storms, emerging from the mist before disappearing beneath the waves.

THE MOURNING STAR A vessel sailors claim appears during violent storms.
THE DROWNED KING A title whispered throughout the coastal kingdoms.
HIS WATERS Sections of the Azure Sea sailors refuse to enter.
“When the sea grows still, listen carefully. The Drowned King may be listening too.”

✦ THE DEEPEST WATERS ✦

THE LEVIATHANS

≋ ✦ ≋ ───── ≋ ✦ ≋

The deepest waters of the Azure Sea are said to be home to enormous creatures known as Leviathans.

They are rarely seen.

Some sailors describe them as enormous serpentine creatures.

Others claim they have wings, horns, or glowing eyes.

Whatever they truly are, most ships avoid the deepest parts of the ocean.

✦ A PHENOMENON OF THE SEA ✦

THE MOON TIDES

≋ ✦ ≋

During certain nights, the Azure Sea glows beneath the moon.

Entire stretches of ocean become illuminated with blue and silver light.

The phenomenon is known as the Moon Tide.

🌙 ≋ 🌊 ≋ 🌙

Nobody knows exactly why it happens. Some believe it is caused by ancient magic beneath the ocean floor.

✦ OUTLAWS OF THE WATER ✦

THE PIRATES OF THE AZURE COAST

≋ ✦ ≋ ───── ≋ ✦ ≋

Piracy has existed along the coast for centuries.

Some pirates are ruthless criminals. Others consider themselves independent sailors who refuse to answer to kingdoms.

Several pirate crews have become legendary throughout Aetheria.

Their hidden coves are scattered among the islands, making them extremely difficult for royal navies to eliminate.

✦ THE TEMPER OF THE SEA ✦

THE STORMS

≋ ───── ⚡ ───── ≋

The Azure Coast is famous for sudden magical storms.

Lightning may strike the ocean without producing thunder.

Waves can rise high enough to swallow ships.

Sailors sometimes report seeing strange lights moving inside the clouds.

THE SAILOR'S WARNING When the sea changes color, turn back.
