✦ THE WILDLANDS ✦

The Untamed World

✦

The Wildlands are the vast, untamed territories stretching across much of Aetheria.

Forests, marshes, mountains, valleys, rivers, cliffs, ancient ruins, and unexplored wilderness make up a region larger than many kingdoms.

There are no true borders here.

No king rules all of it.

No army has mapped every path.

And there are places where no human has ever set foot.

The Wildlands belong to the creatures and peoples who have lived there long before kingdoms drew lines across maps.

✦ THE ROGUE KNIGHT ✦

COLESIN HARTLEY

THE EMERALD REAPER

✦ ───────── ✦

Born between two worlds. Claimed by neither.

Colesin Hartley was born to a human mother and a Panther Demi-Human father.

Because of his mixed heritage, he was never completely accepted by either side.

Yet the Wildlands became the one place where he never needed to choose.

He learned its forests, rivers, cliffs, hidden paths, creatures, and dangers.

“The Wildlands don't belong to kings.”

Colesin eventually became a Royal Knight. But the longer he served the kingdoms, the more he witnessed their treatment of the Wildlands and its people.

Eventually, he abandoned the crown.

THE NAME THEY GAVE HIM THE EMERALD REAPER

Among the people of the Wildlands, Colesin became something more than a rogue knight.

He became a protector.

Those who hunt demi-humans, steal from ancient ruins, or prey upon the creatures of the wilderness have learned to fear his name.

✦ THE DRAGON BOND ✦

VAELITH

✦ ───── ✦

Colesin's greatest companion is Vaelith, an ancient black-and-emerald dragon.

Their connection is more than companionship.

They share a Dragon Bond.

Through the bond, Colesin can sense Vaelith's presence, emotions, and danger.

🐉

When an enormous shadow passes over the forest canopy, the people of the Wildlands know better than to look for an enemy.

They know who is watching.

✦ BEYOND THE MAPS ✦

THE DEEP WILDS

✦ ───── ✦

Beyond the known territories lies a region simply called the Deep Wilds.

Few maps even acknowledge its existence.

Those who have ventured inside describe enormous forests, strange weather, ancient ruins, and creatures unlike anything found elsewhere.

“If you were meant to know, you would already know.”
🌳

✦ THE OLDEST SECRET ✦

THE HEART OF THE WILDS

✦ ───── ✦

At the center of the deepest wilderness lies a place known only through legend.

The Heart of the Wilds.

Nobody knows exactly where it is.

Some believe it is an enormous ancient tree. Others believe it is a magical spring. Some believe it is an entire hidden kingdom.

Whatever it is, the Wildlands seem to remember it.
