✦ THE WORLD ✦

Where the Wild Still Remembers

✦

Aetheria is a land older than the kingdoms that claim it. Forests swallow forgotten roads, mountains guard secrets beneath their snow, and magic moves through the earth like a living pulse.

Humans, demi-humans, dragons, knights, and things far older share a world where the boundaries between legend and reality have never truly been clear.

“The land does not forget. It only waits.”
