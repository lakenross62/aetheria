✦ THE VERDANT PLAINS ✦

Where the Wind Runs Free

✦

The Verdant Plains are a vast region of rolling grasslands stretching across the central lands of Aetheria.

Endless fields of emerald grass, golden wheat, wildflowers, ancient trees, winding rivers, and distant hills cover the landscape.

Unlike the dense forests of the Wildlands or the dangerous wetlands of the Marshlands, the Verdant Plains are open and expansive.

The horizon seems almost endless.

Travelers can see storms approaching from miles away.

Peaceful does not mean harmless.

Bandits, wild beasts, ancient ruins, and remnants of forgotten wars can still be found throughout the region.

✦ THE OPEN FIELDS ✦

THE GOLDEN SEA

✦ ───────── ✦

Deep within the Verdant Plains lies a massive stretch of tall golden grass known as the Golden Sea.

During the summer months, the grass grows so tall that smaller travelers can disappear completely within it.

When the wind moves across the fields, the grass ripples like waves upon an ocean.

Ⅱ

Ⅲ

“The fields move like an ocean when the wind arrives.”

✦ CREATURES OF THE PLAINS ✦

THE WILD HERDS

“Some horses choose their riders.”

✦ ───── ✦

Large herds of wild horses roam some of the most remote parts of the Plains.

These horses are known for their speed, intelligence, and incredible endurance.

Some possess unusual markings or naturally magical traits.

🐎

Taming one is considered extremely difficult.

Certain Plains communities have developed deep relationships with the wild herds and refuse to hunt them.

✦ ANCIENT PATHS ✦

THE WINDROADS

✦ ───── ✦

The Verdant Plains contain an ancient network of roads known as the Windroads.

Some roads are still used by merchants and travelers. Others have almost completely disappeared beneath the grass.

The oldest Windroads were built long before many of the current kingdoms existed.

🌾

Some roads seem to lead toward places that no longer exist.

Nobody knows who built the oldest roads.

🪨

✦ WATERWAYS OF AETHERIA ✦

THE SILVER RIVER

✦

The Silver River is one of the largest waterways flowing through the Verdant Plains.

Its clear waters travel from the northern regions before winding through the grasslands and eventually reaching the Azure Coast.

The river provides drinking water, irrigation, transportation, and fishing.

✦ THE ROGUE KNIGHT ✦

COLESIN HARTLEY

THE EMERALD REAPER

✦ ───── ✦

The Verdant Plains are one of the regions Colesin Hartley has traveled through during his years outside the kingdoms.

As a former Royal Knight and now a rogue knight, Colesin is familiar with the old roads, settlements, and dangerous wilderness surrounding the Plains.

He does not consider the Verdant Plains his home.

ROGUE KNIGHT TRAVELER PROTECTOR

However, he understands the people who live there.

He respects communities that protect themselves without relying on kingdoms or nobles.

The Plains also provide something Colesin rarely finds elsewhere in Aetheria:

Space.

The endless fields allow him to travel for days without entering a city or answering to a kingdom.

Vaelith, his ancient dragon companion, is also known to occasionally fly across the skies above the Plains.

When the enormous dragon's shadow crosses the fields, villagers usually know to look upward.
