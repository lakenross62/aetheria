✦ THE ASHEN LANDS ✦

Where the Earth Still Burns

✦

The Ashen Lands are a vast and dangerous region located within the Wildlands of Aetheria.

Once covered in forests, valleys, and thriving settlements, the region was transformed by an ancient catastrophe.

Today, blackened earth stretches beneath skies stained with smoke and ash. Dead trees rise from the ground, while volcanic mountains dominate the horizon.

Rivers of molten stone cut through the landscape, and ancient ruins remain buried beneath layers of ash.

The Ashen Lands are not dead.

Something beneath the earth is still burning.

✦ ANCIENT HISTORY ✦

THE GREAT BURNING

✦ ───────── ✦

Thousands of years ago, the Ashen Lands were nothing like they are today.

Ancient forests covered the region, rivers flowed freely, and several civilizations flourished.

Then something happened.

Ancient records describe a catastrophic event involving fire, earth, and powerful magic.

Ⅰ

The Burning

A wave of unnatural fire swept across the land, consuming everything in its path.

Ⅱ

The Ashfall

Ash covered the region for years, turning fertile land into blackened earth.

Ⅲ

The Silence

Entire settlements disappeared, leaving only ruins beneath the ash.

“The fire did not come from the sky.”

🌫️

Ash Clouds

✦ CREATURES OF THE ASHES ✦

FIRE-BORN CREATURES

Not everything survived the burning unchanged.

✦ ───── ✦

The creatures of the Ashen Lands have adapted to an environment where ordinary life struggles to survive.

Ash wolves hunt beneath smoke-filled skies. Ember drakes nest within volcanic caves. Salamander-like creatures move through areas of extreme heat.

⚠

The deeper regions contain creatures that have never been properly documented.

Some are harmless.

Others are capable of surviving directly within volcanic environments.

✦ ANCIENT POWER ✦

MAGIC OF THE ASHEN LANDS

✦ ───── ✦

Magic within the Ashen Lands is strongly connected to fire, earth, destruction, and transformation.

Fire mages often travel into the region to study its unusual magical energy.

Certain areas contain unusually concentrated magical power.

🔥

Spells involving fire and earth can become significantly more powerful within certain parts of the Ashen Lands.

But the magic is unstable.
The land does not always obey those who use it.

🏛️

✦ LOST CIVILIZATIONS ✦

THE BLACK CITIES

✦ ───── ✦

Throughout the Ashen Lands are the remains of ancient cities destroyed during the Great Burning.

Their black stone buildings have survived beneath layers of ash and volcanic rock.

WHO BUILT THEM? Unknown.
WHY WERE THEY DESTROYED? Unknown.
WHAT LIES BENEATH THEM? Unknown.
“The oldest doors are always the ones that remain warm.”

✦ A TRADITION REMEMBERED ✦

THE ASHEN NIGHT

✦ ───── ✦

Every year, communities near the Ashen Lands observe a night of silence.

No fires are lit.

No weapons are forged.

No mining is permitted.

The reason has been forgotten.

The tradition remains.

THE OLD WARNING

DO NOT WAKE THE FIRE

Elders say the Ashen Lands were not destroyed by fire alone.

⚒️

✦ THE FORGOTTEN ART ✦

THE ASHEN FORGE

WEAPONS OF FIRE

✦ ───── ✦

Deep within the Ashen Lands are ancient forges built directly above volcanic vents.

Weapons created within these forges are believed to retain traces of the fire that once destroyed the region.

FIRE STRENGTH TRANSFORMATION

Some weapons forged here have survived for thousands of years without rusting or breaking.

The secrets of these ancient forges are carefully protected by the smithing clans.

Every forge has a flame.
The oldest ones are said to burn without fuel.
