✦ THE LEGEND OF THE IRON VALKYRIE ✦

A Woman Who Became a Warning

✦

Eirwen Vale is one of the most infamous figures of the Mountain Ranges.

To travelers, she is a warning.

To mountain villages, she is a protector.

To invading armies, she is a nightmare.

For generations, stories have been told about a woman with enormous wings who watches the mountains from the highest cliffs.

They say she can hear footsteps through a snowstorm. They say she can see travelers from miles away. They say she can disappear into a blizzard without leaving a trace.

“The mountains may belong to no kingdom. But they are under the protection of the Iron Valkyrie.”

✦ HER BLOODLINES ✦

Born of Two Mountain Bloodlines

🦉

Owl Bloodline

From her mother, Eirwen inherited enormous feathered wings, exceptional night vision, sensitive hearing, silent flight, extraordinary eyesight, and powerful hunting instincts.

🐐

Mountain Goat Bloodline

From her father, she inherited powerful legs, exceptional balance, incredible climbing ability, strong endurance, resistance to extreme cold, curved horns, and dark cloven hooves.

Some considered her a blessing. Others considered her an omen. Eirwen considered herself neither.

✦ THE WOMAN BENEATH THE LEGEND ✦

Appearance

Silver Eyes

Pale silver eyes with faint golden undertones resemble moonlight reflecting across freshly fallen snow.

Owl Features

Fine downy feathers blend naturally into the skin around her temples, cheeks, and jawline.

Chestnut Hair

Thick, wavy chestnut-brown hair falls past her shoulders, scattered with lighter strands.

Mountain Horns

Two small curved goat horns emerge from beneath her hair.

Powerful Wings

Enormous wings carry cream, warm brown, charcoal, and muted gray feathers patterned like a mountain owl.

Warrior's Build

Strong legs, defined arms, powerful shoulders, and a narrow waist reflect a lifetime spent climbing and fighting.

✦ THE WINGS OF THE VALKYRIE ✦

Born to Rule the Sky

Eirwen's wings are enormous, powerful, and almost completely silent.

When fully extended, they allow her to glide across entire mountain valleys. Their natural coloring allows her to blend into snow, stone, and clouds.

During storms, Eirwen can disappear almost completely into the sky.

Her wings are not merely weapons. They are part of who she is.

✦ THE WEAPON OF THE VALKYRIE ✦

Ironfall

⚔

A long, elegant mountain-forged spear.

Ironfall was forged from Star-Iron recovered from the mountain. Its dark metal contains faint silver veins and never rusts.

Eirwen uses it in close combat and while fighting from the air. She can descend from extreme heights with Ironfall pointed downward, striking with enough force to crack stone.

The kingdom tried to steal the mountain's treasure. That treasure became the weapon protecting its people.

✦ THE FALL OF HER OLD LIFE ✦

The Night the Mountain Burned

I

The Village

Eirwen was born in a small settlement hidden high within the mountains, surrounded by Owl and Goat Demi-Human families.

II

Star-Iron

A powerful human kingdom discovered an ancient magical mineral beneath the mountain and demanded it.

III

The Attack

The mountain people refused. The kingdom sent soldiers. Eirwen's father was killed defending the village, and her mother died attempting to save the children.

IV

The Avalanche

At sixteen, Eirwen used her knowledge of the mountain to trigger an enormous avalanche that swallowed the invading army.

V

The Legend

Eirwen never returned to ordinary life. She remained in the mountains and began protecting the remaining settlements.

✦ HER CONSTANT COMPANION ✦

Veyra

The owl who never left her side.

🦉

Veyra

Great Mountain Owl • Female • 17 Years

Veyra is a large female mountain owl with snowy cream, muted gray, warm brown, and charcoal feathers.

She possesses unusually strong intelligence and understands spoken language remarkably well.

Eirwen found Veyra injured after she became trapped in an abandoned hunting snare. Eirwen freed her and nursed her back to health.

Veyra never left.

She is not Eirwen's pet. She is her companion, scout, hunting partner, and closest family.
