✦ THE NORTHERN SNOWLANDS ✦

Where Winter Reigns

✦

The Northern Snowlands are a vast frozen region located at the northernmost edge of Aetheria.

Endless fields of snow stretch toward distant mountains, while frozen forests, glaciers, icy rivers, and enormous frozen lakes dominate the land.

Winter rules the region for most of the year.

Temperatures can fall far below freezing, especially during the long northern nights.

Blizzards can appear without warning.

And beyond the snowline lie places where few outsiders have ever survived.

The people who call the Snowlands home have learned to respect the cold rather than attempt to conquer it.

THE REGIONS OF THE SNOWLANDS

✦ ───────── ✦

The Northern Snowlands contain several distinct environments, each shaped by the endless winter.

❄️

The Frozen Plains

Endless fields of snow and ice where powerful northern winds sweep across the land.

🌲

The Frostwood

Ancient evergreen forests covered in permanent frost and heavy snow.

🏔️

The Ice Mountains

Towering mountains covered in glaciers and ancient frozen caves.

🌊

The Frozen Lakes

Enormous lakes whose surfaces remain frozen throughout most of the year.

🧊

The Great Glacier

A massive field of ancient ice believed to contain secrets buried beneath its surface.

🌙

The Far North

The most remote region of the Snowlands, where almost nothing is known.

✦ THE WINTER ✦

THE CLIMATE OF THE NORTH

✦

The Snowlands experience some of the harshest weather in all of Aetheria.

❄️

Endless Snow

Snow covers much of the northern landscape throughout the year.

🌨️

Blizzards

Powerful storms can completely erase trails and make travel nearly impossible.

🌙

The Long Night

During winter, darkness can last for extremely long periods of time.

☀️

The Pale Sun

Even during daylight, the northern sun often provides little warmth.

✦ THE NORTHERN LIGHTS ✦

THE AURORA

✦ ───── ✦

On certain nights, brilliant ribbons of light appear across the northern sky.

The people of the Snowlands call this phenomenon the Aurora.

Some believe the lights are created by ancient magic.

Others believe they are spirits traveling across the heavens.

Whatever the truth may be, the northern people consider the Aurora sacred.

✦ THE OLDEST CREATURES ✦

THE FROST DRAGONS

✦ ───── ✦

Ancient Frost Dragons are believed to have lived within the northern mountains for thousands of years.

Unlike many dragons farther south, Frost Dragons rarely interact with humans.

Their enormous nests are hidden inside glaciers and frozen mountain caverns.

🐉

Northern villages leave offerings near certain mountain caves in hopes of keeping sleeping dragons undisturbed.

Nobody knows how many dragons sleep beneath the ice.

✦ BEYOND THE KNOWN NORTH ✦

THE WHITE SILENCE

✦ ───── ✦

Beyond the settled regions lies an enormous expanse of untouched snow.

It is known simply as the White Silence.

There are no permanent roads. Few landmarks exist. Even experienced travelers can become lost.

“When the snow takes your footprints, turn back.”
❄️

✦ THE OLDEST SECRET ✦

THE HEART OF WINTER

✦ ───── ✦

Deep within the northern wilderness lies a place known only through ancient stories.

The Heart of Winter.

Nobody knows exactly where it is.

Some believe it is the source of all northern magic. Others believe it is an ancient frozen temple.

Some believe an ancient being sleeps beneath it.

Whatever lies there, the people of the north do not seek it.

THE LAWS OF THE SNOWLANDS

✦ ───── ✦

02 — Never enter an unknown glacier cave.

03 — Never disturb a sleeping creature.

04 — Never follow strange lights across the snow.

05 — Always respect northern shrines.

06 — Never assume frozen ice is safe.

07 — If the wind suddenly stops, turn back.
