✦ THE MAN BEHIND THE LEGEND ✦

COLESIN HARTLEY

✦

Colesin Hartley is a former Royal Knight who abandoned the Crown after discovering the corruption hidden beneath the kingdom he once swore to protect.

NAME Colesin Hartley
AGE 25
HEIGHT 6'5" / 196 cm
HERITAGE Human / Demi-Human Hybrid
FORMER ROLE Royal Knight
CURRENT ROLE Rogue Knight
TITLE The Emerald Reaper
COMPANION Vaelith

✦ BLOOD & BEGINNINGS ✦

THE FORBIDDEN BLOODLINE

✦ ───────── ✦

Colesin was born from the forbidden relationship between a human woman and a Panther Demi-Human man.

Their relationship was considered unacceptable by the royal society surrounding them.

Colesin grew up knowing that his heritage made him different in the eyes of many people, but his parents taught him that blood did not determine a person's worth.

“Judge a person by what they choose to become, not by the blood that created them.”

Those words became one of the foundations of Colesin's entire worldview.

He would eventually carry that belief onto battlefields, into royal halls, and against the very kingdom he once called home.

✦ THE WARRIOR ✦

THE FACE OF THE REAPER

✦

Colesin possesses a naturally commanding presence that often makes people notice him before he speaks.

Standing at 6'5", he has a tall, muscular, heavily trained warrior's physique.

Years of knightly training, wilderness survival, and combat have made him powerful, agile, durable, and exceptionally dangerous when forced into battle.

🖤

Obsidian Hair

Short black shag hair with two pointed tufts created entirely by his hairstyle.

💚

Emerald Eyes

Intense neon emerald-green eyes known for their unnaturally striking appearance.

⚔️

Warrior's Build

A powerful physique shaped by years of training and combat.

🩸

Panther Heritage

Enhanced agility, reflexes, balance, hearing, night vision, endurance, and hunting instincts.

Despite his heritage, Colesin remains human-presenting.

He has normal human ears hidden beneath his hair. He has no animal ears and no tail.

✦ BEFORE THE REAPER ✦

THE ROYAL KNIGHT

✦ ───── ✦

Colesin eventually became a Royal Knight.

For years, he genuinely believed he was protecting the kingdom and serving an oath worth keeping.

He believed that the Crown represented order, justice, and protection.

He trained relentlessly and became one of the kingdom's most capable warriors.

⚔ DISCIPLINE

Years of military and knightly training.

🛡 DUTY

A genuine desire to protect innocent people.

♜ LOYALTY

Faith in the oath he believed he had sworn to something honorable.

“I swore to protect the people. I never swore to worship the throne.”

✦ THE TRUTH ✦

THE CROWN'S BETRAYAL

✦ ───── ✦

The Crown eventually discovered the truth about Colesin's parents.

Rather than accepting their relationship, the royal family ordered his parents killed.

Colesin eventually discovered what had truly happened.

The kingdom he had sworn to protect had destroyed the family that had raised him.

The realization shattered everything he believed about the Crown.

THE OATH BROKE NOT HIS PRINCIPLES.

✦ THE FALL OF A KNIGHT ✦

THE ROGUE KNIGHT

✦

Colesin abandoned the Crown.

He abandoned his oath.

He walked away from the title of Royal Knight and everything that came with it.

The Crown branded him a criminal, traitor, rogue, threat, and enemy of the kingdom.

Colesin accepted the consequences.

He no longer served the people sitting upon the throne.

He served the people crushed beneath it.

💚

✦ THE LEGEND ✦

THE EMERALD REAPER

✦ ───── ✦

Colesin earned the legendary title “The Emerald Reaper” during his years as a Royal Knight.

His emerald eyes became associated with a warrior who could appear upon a battlefield like a shadow and leave entire enemy formations defeated.

He was fast.

Precise.

Relentless when necessary.

But the title eventually became something greater than a battlefield nickname.

THE CROWN CALLS HIM TRAITOR. THE OPPRESSED CALL HIM PROTECTOR.

To those who have suffered beneath corrupt authority, the Emerald Reaper became a symbol of defiance.

✦ THE ONE WHO CHOSE HIM ✦

VAELITH

✦ ───── ✦

INTELLIGENT INDEPENDENT PROUD LOYAL PROTECTIVE OBSERVANT

Vaelith never considered himself Colesin's servant.

He chose Colesin as his equal.

✦ AFTER THE FALL ✦

THE ROGUE'S PATH

✦

Colesin never regretted leaving the Crown.

He chose a life without a kingdom rather than return to a system he believed was corrupt.

He became a wandering protector.

He helps those who cannot protect themselves, challenges corrupt authority, and refuses to judge people solely by their blood or heritage.

His life is difficult.

His enemies are numerous.

But he is free.

And for Colesin, freedom is worth the price.
