✦ THE MOUNTAIN RANGES ✦

Where the Sky Touches the Earth

✦

The Mountain Ranges are a vast region of towering peaks stretching through the heart of Aetheria.

Ancient mountains rise above the clouds, their highest summits permanently covered in snow. Deep valleys divide the range, while enormous cliffs, hidden caves, frozen rivers, and forgotten ruins disappear into the wilderness.

The mountains are beautiful.

They are also unforgiving.

Few outsiders understand how dangerous they truly are. A traveler can become lost within minutes.

A peaceful-looking trail can lead directly into the territory of a mountain beast.

And some peaks have never been successfully climbed.

✦ ANCIENT HISTORY ✦

THE OLDEST LAND

✦ ───────── ✦

The Mountain Ranges are believed to be among the oldest regions of Aetheria.

Long before modern kingdoms established borders, civilizations lived among these peaks.

Ⅰ

Temples

Ancient places of worship carved directly into the mountainsides.

Ⅱ

Fortresses

Forgotten strongholds swallowed by stone and snow.

Ⅲ

Unknown Ruins

Structures containing symbols no modern Aetherian scholar can translate.

“They simply don't believe humans were the first ones to live there.”

✦ RARE RESOURCE ✦

STAR-IRON

Metal that fell from the heavens.

✦ ───── ✦

According to ancient stories, fragments of Star-Iron fell from the heavens thousands of years ago.

The metal is nearly impossible to destroy. Weapons forged from Star-Iron can hold magical enchantments exceptionally well.

⚠

Mining Star-Iron is extremely dangerous. Many miners never return.

The mountain clans consider the deepest deposits sacred.

✦ ABOVE THE CLOUDS ✦

THE DRAGON PEAKS

✦ ───── ✦

Ancient dragons are believed to have nested here long before humans arrived in Aetheria.

Some dragons still live within the mountains. Most avoid civilization.

There are stories of enormous dragons sleeping beneath glaciers.

🕯️

Mountain villagers leave offerings near certain caves to ensure the creatures remain undisturbed.

Nobody has ever confirmed whether the offerings actually work.
Nobody is willing to stop making them.

👁️

✦ THE UNKNOWN ✦

THE WATCHERS

✦ ───── ✦

Enormous stone statues have been found on several mountain peaks.

They depict unknown humanoid figures with wings, horns, and strange weapons.

WHO BUILT THEM? Unknown.
WHY DO THEY WATCH? Unknown.
WHY DO THEY FACE ONE MOUNTAIN? Unknown.
“They simply avoid the statues after sunset.”

✦ A TRAGEDY REMEMBERED ✦

THE NIGHT OF
THE BROKEN PEAK

✦ ───── ✦

A kingdom attempted to seize a small mixed demi-human settlement after discovering Star-Iron beneath the mountain.

The settlement was home to Owl and Goat demi-human families.

The villagers refused.

The soldiers attacked.

The settlement was destroyed.

THE SURVIVOR

EIRWEN VALE

Only one young survivor became famous afterward.

🦉

✦ THE LIVING LEGEND ✦

EIRWEN VALE

THE IRON VALKYRIE

✦ ───── ✦

Eirwen returned to the mountain years later.

She became its guardian.

PROTECTOR WARNING LEGEND

Some villages leave food outside their homes during particularly harsh winters.

Not because they believe Eirwen needs it. Because they believe the gesture shows respect.

Some hunters claim she has saved them. Others swear she chased them away.

Children tell stories about seeing her flying above the clouds.

Adults insist they've never seen her.

Eirwen herself doesn't care what people call her.
She simply continues watching the mountains.
