✦ THE GREAT DIVIDE ✦

Where Aetheria Was Torn Apart

✦

The Great Divide is one of the most dangerous and mysterious regions within Aetheria.

A colossal fracture stretches across the land, separating enormous cliffs, mountains, valleys, and deep ravines.

The region is difficult to cross and even more difficult to understand.

Ancient ruins can be found along the cliffs, while strange magical energy flows through the deepest portions of the Divide.

The Divide is more than a scar upon the land.

It is a reminder of something that happened long before the kingdoms of Aetheria existed.

✦ ANCIENT HISTORY ✦

THE GREAT FRACTURE

✦ ───────── ✦

Long before modern civilizations rose across Aetheria, the land surrounding the Great Divide was whole.

Ancient forests stretched across the region. Rivers crossed the valleys, and civilizations flourished along the mountains.

Then the earth split.

Ancient records describe a tremendous magical event that caused the land itself to fracture.

Ⅰ

The Breaking

The earth violently split apart, creating the enormous chasm known today as the Great Divide.

Ⅱ

The Collapse

Mountains and ancient structures collapsed into the newly formed depths.

Ⅲ

The Silence

Whatever caused the catastrophe disappeared, leaving the truth behind it buried beneath the earth.

“The earth did not crack. It was broken.”

✦ THE LANDSCAPE ✦

A World Split in Two

⛰️

The Rift Cliffs

Towering cliffs surround the deepest sections of the Divide, making passage extremely dangerous.

🌫️

The Lower Mist

Thick mist frequently fills the deepest portions of the chasm, hiding what lies beneath.

🪨

Shattered Peaks

Broken mountain ranges rise from both sides of the Divide, forming an enormous natural barrier.

🌌

The Deep Rift

The lowest regions remain largely unexplored. Strange lights can sometimes be seen far below the surface.

✦ CREATURES OF THE DIVIDE ✦

CREATURES OF THE RIFT

The deeper the Divide goes, the stranger its inhabitants become.

✦ ───── ✦

The cliffs and valleys surrounding the Great Divide are home to countless creatures.

Cliff wolves roam the upper regions. Giant birds nest within the mountains. Rock-scaled reptiles inhabit the deeper caves and ravines.

⚠

Creatures living within the deepest portions of the Rift have rarely been observed.

Some hunters claim that enormous creatures move beneath the mist.

No reliable record has ever confirmed what they are.

✦ ANCIENT POWER ✦

MAGIC OF THE GREAT DIVIDE

✦ ───── ✦

Magic within the Great Divide is unusually unstable.

Ancient magical energy appears to flow through the stone and beneath the deepest parts of the Rift.

Mages who study the region commonly associate its magic with earth, space, gravity, and ancient enchantments.

⚡

Magical spells may behave differently within the deepest parts of the Divide.

The Rift changes the magic around it.
And nobody knows why.

🏛️

✦ LOST CIVILIZATIONS ✦

THE RUINS OF THE RIFT

Ancient structures can be found throughout the cliffs surrounding the Great Divide.

WHO BUILT THEM? Unknown.

✦ A MYSTERY REMEMBERED ✦

THE RIFT THAT NEVER HEALED

✦ ───── ✦

The people living around the Divide have passed down stories about the great fracture for generations.

Some believe the Rift was created by ancient gods.

Others believe powerful mages tore the earth apart.

Some believe the land itself was protecting Aetheria from something beneath it.

Nobody knows which story is true.

Perhaps none of them are.

THE OLDEST WARNING

DO NOT CROSS WHERE THE EARTH ENDS

Those who live closest to the Divide say that some paths should never be followed.

⚔️

✦ THE WARRIORS OF THE DIVIDE ✦

THE RIFT GUARDIANS

KEEPERS OF THE EDGE

✦ ───── ✦

For generations, warriors have been tasked with protecting the settlements and ancient ruins surrounding the Great Divide.

They patrol dangerous paths, protect travelers, and prevent treasure hunters from disturbing certain ancient sites.

STRENGTH DUTY RESOLVE

Their greatest responsibility is keeping people away from areas believed to be unstable.

Few willingly travel into the deepest Rift.

The Divide cannot be guarded completely.
But someone must stand at its edge.
