✦ THE MARSHLANDS ✦

Where the Water Remembers

✦

The Marshlands are a vast wetland region located within the Wildlands of Aetheria.

Endless reeds, twisted trees, dark waterways, enormous lily fields, and thick blankets of mist cover the region.

Ancient roots rise from the muddy water, while forgotten ruins slowly disappear beneath vines and moss.

The Marshlands are beautiful from a distance.

Up close, they are unpredictable.

Paths can disappear overnight. Rivers can change direction. Fog can roll in without warning.

And the deeper someone travels into the wetlands, the less certain they can be that the path ahead is the same one they entered through.

✦ THE EVER-PRESENT FOG ✦

THE MARSH MIST

✦ ───────── ✦

The Marshlands are almost always covered in some amount of mist.

During the morning, thin fog floats above the waterways. After sunset, it becomes much thicker and can completely hide the surrounding landscape.

Some of the mist is believed to contain traces of ancient magic.

01

Distant Voices

Travelers sometimes hear voices coming from places where nobody stands.

02

Strange Lights

Unfamiliar lights drift through the fog and vanish without explanation.

03

Lost Paths

Travelers may find themselves standing exactly where they began.

04

Figures

Unfamiliar silhouettes sometimes appear through the mist.

“The people who live in the Marshlands have learned not to trust everything they see within the mist.”

✦ THE DARK WATER ✦

THE BLACKWATER

✦ ───── ✦

Throughout the Marshlands are enormous pools of unnaturally dark water known as the Blackwater.

Nobody knows exactly how deep some of them are.

The water remains strangely warm even during colder seasons, and faint blue-green lights can sometimes be seen moving beneath the surface.

☽

Fish and other aquatic creatures live within the Blackwater, but locals rarely swim in the deeper pools.

Some believe something much older is hiding beneath it.

✦ A SACRED PLACE ✦

THE MOONLILY FIELDS

✦ ───── ✦

Deep within the Marshlands lies a massive field of magical Moonlilies.

During the day, the flowers remain closed. When the moon rises, thousands of them bloom simultaneously.

Their glow illuminates the surrounding water with a soft silver light.

RESPECT THE FIELDS

Taking large quantities of Moonlilies is considered extremely disrespectful by the local communities.

✦ LIFE ABOVE THE WATER ✦

MARSH VILLAGES

✦

Most settlements are built above the water.

Wooden walkways connect homes, markets, storage buildings, and gathering areas.

Some villages are built upon enormous natural roots. Others sit on small islands surrounded by deep water.

Lanterns containing glowing insects or magical crystals illuminate the walkways at night.

🏡

Hidden Homes

Many communities deliberately avoid appearing on maps.

🏮

Lantern Walkways

Glowing lanterns guide residents through the darkness.

🌿

Hidden Paths

Local people know routes that outsiders will never find.

Being difficult to find is often safer.

✦ THE LOST CITY ✦

VEYLORA

✦

One of the largest ruins in the Marshlands is the ancient city of Veylora.

Once a thriving civilization, most of the city now lies beneath the wetlands.

Only portions of its towers and temples remain above the water.

The cause of Veylora's destruction is unknown.

Ⅰ

Magical Disaster

Some believe the city was destroyed by an unknown magical catastrophe.

Ⅱ

The Marsh

Others believe the wetlands slowly swallowed the civilization.

Ⅲ

Water Magic

Ancient inscriptions suggest Veylora possessed advanced water magic.

✦ OUTSIDERS IN THE WETLANDS ✦

THE HUNTERS

✦ ───── ✦

The Marshlands have a long history of conflict with outside hunters.

Rare plants, magical creatures, valuable materials, and demi-human traits have attracted hunters from surrounding kingdoms for centuries.

⚠

Many Marshland communities maintain hidden entrances and concealed pathways specifically to protect themselves from hunters.

Travelers who enter the wetlands without permission may find themselves watched long before they realize anyone is nearby.

✦ BEYOND THE KNOWN WETLANDS ✦

THE DEEP MARSH

✦ ───── ✦

The deepest section of the wetlands is known simply as the Deep Marsh.

It is largely unexplored.

THE TREES Grow enormous.
THE WATERWAYS Become darker.
THE MIST Becomes thicker.
THE SOUNDS Become unfamiliar.
“Even experienced Marshland travelers avoid going too deep.”

✦ THE WAY OF THE WETLANDS ✦

MARSHLAND CULTURE

✦

The people of the wetlands generally value community and respect for nature.

FAMILY COMMUNITY NATURE PATIENCE RESPECT SELF-SUFFICIENCY PROTECTION

They believe the land should be respected rather than conquered.

Taking only what is needed is considered an important tradition.

Many communities leave small offerings of food, flowers, or herbs near ancient trees and waterways.

✦ SURVIVAL TRADITION ✦

RULES OF THE MARSHLANDS

✦ ───── ✦

01 — Never drink from unfamiliar water.

02 — Never follow strange lights into the fog.

03 — Never take more plants than you need.

04 — Never disturb ancient ruins.

05 — Never wander away from a marked path during heavy mist.

06 — Never assume still water is empty.
